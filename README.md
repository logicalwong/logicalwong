## 🌟 Featured Projects

### 1. 🔍 **Startup Health & Funding Readiness Agent**
> **Status:** 🚧 In Development
> **Tech Stack:** LangGraph, RAG, LLMs, Vector DB, Document Parsing
> **Description:** Multi-agent system that interviews founders, analyzes business plans, and provides objective, score-based assessments against specific incubation program criteria. Transforms manual, time-intensive processes into automated, scalable workflows.

### 2. 📊 **ESG Investment Intelligence & Reporting Agent**
> **Status:** 🚧 In Development
> **Tech Stack:** RAG, Multi-Agent Orchestration, Financial APIs, Unstructured.io
> **Description:** Automates extraction of ESG metrics from unstructured company data and generates reports aligned with SASB, GRI, and TCFD frameworks. Empowers investors with real-time sustainability intelligence.

### 3. 📝 **Smart Stakeholder Communication Hub**
> **Status:** 📋 Planning
> **Tech Stack:** API Integrations, LLMs, Workflow Automation
> **Description:** Centralized system that ingests project communications (emails, Slack, meeting notes) and automatically produces tailored status reports for executives, team members, clients, and investors—reducing reporting time by 70%.

### 4. 🌐 **Web3 Regulatory Risk Radar**
> **Status:** 💡 Conceptual
> **Tech Stack:** Web Scraping, LLM Analysis, Alert Systems
> **Description:** Real-time monitoring system for FinTech and Web3 regulatory changes with automated impact assessment capabilities. Keeps stakeholders ahead of compliance requirements.
